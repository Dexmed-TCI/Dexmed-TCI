Bolus / loading

The study's TCI pump limited dexmedetomidine to 6 µg/kg/h (10 µg/kg/h for the 6 and 8 ng/mL targets) to avoid bolus-related hypertension and reflex bradycardia. In rate-limited mode the step-down infusions are also kept at or below this cap.

Inputs are locked while live tracking is running. Stop tracking to change them.

Live tracking

Follows the schedule below in real time: what the pump should be set to now, the predicted plasma concentration, deviation from the TCI model and time to the next change. Start it when the bolus is given, or enter the clock time the infusion started if you're starting it later. Tracking keeps going if the phone sleeps or the app is closed.

Method
  • Model (Table 1, typical values): V1 = 1.78·(WT/70) L; V2 = 30.3·(WT/70) L; V3 = 52.0·(WT/70) L; CL = 0.686·(WT/70)0.75 L/min; Q2 = 2.98·(V2/30.3)0.75 L/min; Q3 = 0.602·(V3/52.0)0.75 L/min. Weight was the only covariate; age, height and sex are used only for range warnings.
  • TCI reference: plasma-targeted. The three-compartment system is solved exactly (matrix exponential) in 1-second steps. Each second the controller picks the rate that puts Cp exactly on target at the end of that second. In instant-bolus mode V1 × target is given at t = 0; in rate-limited mode the rate is capped and the loading phase runs at the cap until target is reached.
  • Step-down schedule: starts with the same bolus or loading phase as the TCI. Then, working forward, each step picks the constant rate that can be held the longest while the predicted Cp stays within ±accuracy % of the TCI-predicted Cp at every second. Because Cp is linear in the infusion rate, the feasible range of rates is narrowed second by second until no rate satisfies the band. Steps end on whole minutes, and rates are rounded to the pump's mL/h resolution before the duration is fixed, so the rounded values in the table are what was simulated. This greedy longest-step approach gives the minimum (or very near-minimum) number of rate changes.
  • Max deviation is the largest |Cpschedule − CpTCI| / CpTCI within each step, at 1-second resolution.

This app was built for personal use as a TCI simulator and should not be used in patient care or in any clinical context. DO NOT USE THIS APP

This is a decision-support calculator. Predictions are typical (population) values for healthy adult volunteers (18 subjects, 20–70 yr, 51–110 kg, BMI 20.6–29.3 kg/m², targets 1–8 ng/mL). The authors noted the model should be validated in patients before clinical TCI use. Individual concentrations vary considerably (interindividual and interoccasion variability on V1, V3 and CL). Verify every dose and rate independently and titrate to clinical effect and haemodynamics.
